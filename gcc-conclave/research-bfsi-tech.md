## Mindspace business park (Madhapur, HITEC City) notes

- Carrier Digital Hub India: Hyderabad job listings give Raheja Mindspace, Madhapur as the address.
- Mindspace Madhapur tenants named in 2025 coverage include Bank of America (BA Continuum), Qualcomm, Verizon, Cognizant, AMD, L&T and HighRadius. HCA Global Services pre-leased 644,000 sq ft at Mindspace IT Park.
- Qualcomm's 1.8 million sq ft Commerzone Raidurg building was bought by Mindspace REIT in January 2025 (separate from the Madhapur park).
- Most new 2025 to 2026 entrants above sit in Gachibowli, the Financial District, Knowledge City or Kokapet, not Mindspace.
