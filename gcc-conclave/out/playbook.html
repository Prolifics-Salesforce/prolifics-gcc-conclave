Prolifics GCC Conclave, Hyderabad

Built in Hyderabad: from first desk to AI at scale

An invitation only evening for the leaders who built and now run Hyderabad's global capability centers, hosted by Satya Bolli. Twenty guests, all heads of global companies' capability centers in Hyderabad, one seat per company. 1Goals and how we measure them

One evening that puts Satya among the people who build and run Hyderabad's GCCs, and earns a reason to meet each of them again in January.

What success looks like

  • 20 GCC heads in the room on 17 December
  • 12 or more yeses by Friday 4 December, including at least 3 of the 4 founders
  • Every guest the head of their center, not a deputy
  • A 1:1 meeting with Satya booked in January with every guest who asks

What we will not do

  • No Prolifics sales pitch on stage. Satya speaks as a builder among builders
  • No photos or quotes from the roundtable; Chatham House rule after 7 pm
  • No deputies or plus ones: one seat per company
  • No guest named in public without their permission
  • No sales follow up before January
