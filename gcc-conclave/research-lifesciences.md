Prepared 21 November 2026 for the Prolifics GCC conclave (Hyderabad, mid December 2026). Scope: life sciences, pharma, medtech and healthcare GCCs with a Hyderabad center.

Method: web search and fetch of news, company pages and press releases. Every person has at least one 2025 or 2026 source. LinkedIn profile URLs did not surface in search for any of these leaders, so that column reads "not found in search" and should be filled by a direct LinkedIn lookup before outreach.

Confidence key: high = named in a company release or two or more independent 2025 to 2026 sources; medium = one source, an undated company page, or a possible role change; low = thin or conflicting evidence.

## Mindspace (Madhapur) check

- Only one healthcare or life sciences GCC is confirmed for Mindspace: HCA Healthcare. HCA Global Services pre-leased 644,000 sq ft (chargeable) in Building 8, Mindspace IT Park, for 10 years from 1 September 2027. Source: https://www.constructionworld.in/policy-updates-and-economic-news/hca-global-services-pre-leases-644000-sq-ft-at-mindspace/100456 (17 Nov 2026). Its current site is Sattva Knowledge Park, HITEC City.
- Eli Lilly is NOT in Mindspace. Its Hyderabad site is in the Phoenix Equinox building, Gachibowli. Source: https://biospectrumindia.com/news/109/26479/lilly-expands-india-presence-with-inauguration-of-hyderabad-site-to-accelerate-global-digital-innovation.html (4 Aug 2025).
- Amgen is in RMZ Spire, HITEC City; Novartis in Sattva Knowledge City; Sanofi in its own Hitech City campus; Thermo Fisher in Genome Valley. None of these is Mindspace.
- ProPharma (a pharma services firm, not on the priority list) has a Mindspace facility: https://biopharmaapac.com/news/44/7305/propharma-advances-its-india-growth-strategy-with-hyderabad-expansion.html
- The 1.5 million sq ft Mindspace Madhapur Building 1 pre-lease is reported as a banking GCC, not pharma.
- For BMS, MSD, Zoetis, Medtronic, Evernorth, Providence and Optum, no source named the building, so Mindspace tenancy is unconfirmed.

## Company notes

**Eli Lilly.** Announced 9 Jan 2025 as Lilly Capability Centre India (LCCI) Hyderabad, Lilly's second India center after Bengaluru (2016). Inaugurated 4 Aug 2025 by Diogo Rau (EVP and Chief Information and Digital Officer) with Chief Minister Revanth Reddy. 220,000 sq ft in Phoenix Equinox, Gachibowli; 100 staff at opening, plan 1,500. Country head is Winselow Tucker, President and General Manager, Lilly India. In Feb 2026 Lilly also announced a manufacturing and quality hub in Hyderabad. Sources: https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/lilly-to-establish-new-global-capability-centre-in-hyderabad-16647 ; https://biospectrumindia.com/news/109/26479/lilly-expands-india-presence-with-inauguration-of-hyderabad-site-to-accelerate-global-digital-innovation.html ; https://www.business-standard.com/health/eli-lilly-targets-india-as-global-export-hub-126021701531_1.html

**Amgen.** Gullapalli named MD in Dec 2024. Site opened 24 Feb 2025 in HITEC City, USD 200M through 2025, about 524,000 sq ft, capacity up to 3,000. A Genome Valley science and innovation center has since been reported. Sources: https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/amgen-opens-200-million-technology-and-innovation-hub-in-hyderabad-125022401027_1.html ; https://biospectrumindia.com/news/66/25443/amgen-announces-senior-leadership-appointment-to-accelerate-technology-and-innovation-from-india.html

**Novartis.** Novartis Knowledge City opened Dec 2015, largest of the company's corporate centers (job post cites over 8,000 associates). Gullapalli led its build out before leaving for Amgen. Country President and MD is Amitabh Dube. Sources: https://www.novartis.com/in-en/about/india-country-leadership-team ; https://novartis.com.cn/careers/career-search/job/details/req-10038607-head-nocc-hyderabad (Feb 2025)

**Sanofi.** Started 2019 (medical services), campus opened 2024, expansion inaugurated 16 Feb 2026 adding over 2.7 lakh sq m in Hitech City, capacity raised from about 2,600 to about 4,500, multi hundred million investment. Madeleine Roach, EVP Head of Business Operations, is the global sponsor. Archana Subramanya was named Head, R&D India Hub in 2026. Source: Sanofi press release linked in the table.

**Bristol Myers Squibb.** Inaugurated 26 Feb 2024 with CEO Chris Boerner, USD 100M (about Rs 820 to 830 crore), 1,500 plus planned. Leadership is in transition: Dhingra (founding MD, still listed July 2026) and Tekuru (appointed MD Hyderabad, 28 Oct 2026). Confirm with BMS before inviting. Sources: https://www.indianpharmapost.com/news/bristol-myers-squibb-inaugurates-new-us100-million-facility-in-hyderabad-15318 ; https://www.cxodigitalpulse.com/?p=64241

**MSD (Merck).** Tech center opened Feb 2025; CIDO Dave Williams projected about 2,700 staff by end 2025, at least double within a couple of years. Expanded 250,000 sq ft Hitech City workplace inaugurated 10 Sep 2026 with Abhishek Narayan Singh, SVP Digital Human Health. Ritesh Srivastava (Executive Director) attended. Sources: https://biospectrumindia.com/news/102/28451/msd-inaugurates-expanded-hyderabad-tech-centre.html ; https://www.biotecnika.org/2025/02/merck-to-expand-workforce-in-india-as-part-of-its-growth-strategy

**Zoetis.** India Capability Center expansion and new office inaugurated 10 Sep 2024 with CIO Keith Sarbaugh. Raghav promoted from VP and Head to MD in Oct 2025. Sources: https://deccanchronicle.com/business/in-other-news/zoetis-opens-india-capability-centre-in-hyderabad-1822582 ; CXO Digital Pulse link in the table.

**Medtronic.** MEIC in Hyderabad since 2011, Medtronic's largest R&D center outside the US. 2024 expansion to 250,000 sq ft under a planned investment of about Rs 3,000 crore. About 1,600 engineers in 2026, more than 200 US patents supported. Sources: https://news.medtronic.com/Shri-D-Sridhar-Babu,-Honorable-Minister-of-IT,-Industries-Commerce,-Govt-of-Telangana-unveils-the-newly-expanded-Medtronic-Engineering-Innovation-Center-in-Hyderabad (Mar 2024) ; Deccan Chronicle link in the table.

**HCA Healthcare.** First GCC, opened Sep 2025 at Sattva Knowledge Park, HITEC City, USD 75M by end 2025, about 1,100 staff at opening, 3,000 by end 2026. Mindspace Building 8 pre-lease from Sep 2027. Sources in the table.

**Evernorth (Cigna).** First global innovation hub outside the US, opened 19 Nov 2024, inaugurated by Cigna global CIO Noelle Eder. Sources: https://www.projectstoday.com/News/Evernorth-Health-Services-expands-presence-in-Hyderabad-GCC-inaugurated ; Mediainfoline link in the table.

**Providence.** Providence India established Feb 2020, new Hi-Tech City office (about 500,000 sq ft, nine floors) opened Feb 2024. A May 2026 report says Sanjeev Garg joined as Executive Director, date not verified. Source: https://cxotoday.com/press-release/providence-expands-its-healthcare-global-innovation-center-in-india-unveils-new-office-facility-in-hyderabad/ (26 Feb 2024, background only)

**Optum (UnitedHealth Group).** Five India cities including Hyderabad. Krishnan has been a director of Optum Global Solutions (India) since June 2023. Source: https://www.optum.in/about/news/uma-ratnam-krishnan-top-gcc-leader-2026.html

**Thermo Fisher.** Customer Experience Center and Bioprocess Design Center at Genome Valley, opened Nov 2025, Rs 85 to 90 crore, Bioprocess center built with the Telangana government and the Department of Biotechnology. Source in the table.
